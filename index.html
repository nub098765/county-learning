General

Switch the whole app to a darker color scheme.

Use bolder, more distinct colors for correct and incorrect counties.

How loud the correct/incorrect sound effects play.

Start each state's progress table collapsed on the setup screen. You can still show it with the Hide/Show button.

How many counties the "Select your best-known" button checks when choosing counties to exclude. Any number from 1 up.

Clear completed states, mistake history, best-known suggestions, and Study progress.

In-Game

Show states in A-Z order everywhere they're grouped or laid out: the setup screen's exclusion checklist and progress tables, and the maps shown during the game.

Always include the state name alongside the target county in prompts.

Reveal the correct county after 3 wrong guesses. (Doesn't apply to List mode)

Size each state's map based on how much it has going on—Texas gets more room, Delaware gets less—with a thin divider between maps.

WARNING: This might lead to some bugs.

Always use dividers (instead of boxes) when playing with 3 or fewer states.

Set the maximum number of states displayed per row, if arranged in a grid.

For all modes except List, add a stopwatch. For pin modes, register a guess the instant you press down on a county, instead of waiting for you to release.

Specific Modes

Check what you've typed as you type it, instead of waiting for you to press Enter. (Doesn't apply to Verbatim)

Group the county checklist in List mode into a section per state, instead of one mixed alphabetical list.

Require all words to be typed exactly as they appear, including marks like diaereses. While this is on, a row of buttons for the special characters in that round's county names (like ö or the ʻokina) appears under the text box, so you can tap them instead of hunting for them on your keyboard.

Keep accepting “St.” and “Ste.” (or just “St” and “Ste”) for “Saint” and “Sainte” even while the setting above is on. Turn this off to type the whole word too. With the setting above off, they’re always accepted.

More tips
  • Right-click, long-press, or click the magnifying glass in the bottom right of any map to zoom in, which makes small counties much easier to click. Right-click again, tap outside the map, or press Escape to zoom back out.
  • For the pin modes, county equivalents that are too tiny to click reliably, like San Francisco, CA or Kalawao, HI, have an enlarged circle you can click with an arrow pointing to their real location.
  • For the type modes, you must type each county exactly as it is defined by the US Census Bureau, including any apostrophes or dashes. (“St.” and “Ste.” are fine for “Saint” and “Sainte”; see Settings.)
  • Only if you are utterly bumfuzzled should you click Give Up. It ends the game immediately and reveals what you missed.
