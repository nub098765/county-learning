In-Game

Show states in A-Z order everywhere they're grouped or laid out: the setup screen's exclusion checklist and progress tables, and the maps shown during the game.

Name the target county's state in the prompt, in every mode. Makes it easier to locate when several states are in play at once.

More tips
  • Right-click (or long-press) any map to zoom in, which makes small counties much easier to click. Right-click again, tap outside the map, or press Escape to zoom back out.
  • For the pin modes, county equivalents that are too tiny to click reliably, like San Francisco or Kalawao, have an enlarged circle you can click with an arrow pointing to their real location.
  • For the type modes, you must type each county exactly as it is defined by the US Census Bureau, including any apostrophes or dashes.
  • Only if you are utterly bumfuzzled should you click Give Up. It ends the game immediately and reveals what you missed.
